# Episode 1: Building the show (2026-10-19)

**With:** Gabe, Stan, Jimmy (Stan's bot) and, near the end, Rajesh (Gabe's bot)
**Recorded:** a Google Meet, through [Vibe Conferencing](https://vibeconferencing.com). Not streamed: live access was still unlocking.

## What we did

This episode was meta: we built Vibe Camp while recording it.

1. **YouTube.** We made the [VibeCamp Show](https://www.youtube.com/@vibecamp-show) channel, after first trying Stan's bike-jump channel by mistake. We requested live streaming at 09:31; YouTube makes a new channel wait 24 hours. The handle `@vibecamp` was taken, and YouTube rejected the dotted versions.
2. **GitHub.** Gabe created the `VibeCamp-show` org and Jimmy created this repo.
3. **Theme song.** Jimmy wrote the lyrics and drove Suno in Stan's browser. We picked the first take as the more "camp" one; it's in [`music/`](../music).
4. **Logo.** Stan suggested a scout merit badge, and Nano Banana made two options. We picked A, which now appears on the repo, the YouTube channel and the GitHub org.
5. **Format.** See below.

## The format we landed on (loose on purpose)

- **Three fixed beats:** cold open → open-ended build → demo.
- **Every show ends with a repo:** the thing we built, or the guest's project.
- **Plots:** the A-plot is the live build; the B-plot is a friend's show & tell; the C-plot is debugging Vibe Conferencing itself, which runs in the background of every episode.
- This repo is the umbrella, with show notes per episode. Each episode's project gets its own repo in the org, named with a date or episode number.
